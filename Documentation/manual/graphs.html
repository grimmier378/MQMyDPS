Graphs & Charts

The Graphs tab in the main DPS Report window gives you visual representations of your combat performance across battles. Four graph types are available, and you can enable any combination of them.

The Graphs tab showing DPS trends and damage breakdown charts.

Graph Types

DPS Line Graph

A single line tracking your DPS across battles. This is the simplest view — it shows how your damage output varies from fight to fight, making it easy to spot trends or identify when you had a particularly strong or weak battle.

Damage Breakdown

A multi-line graph that splits your damage into categories on separate axes:

  • Left axis — Melee, direct damage, DoT, and pet damage
  • Right axis (top) — Critical strike damage
  • Right axis (bottom) — Healing and critical healing

This chart helps you understand the composition of your damage over time. For example, you might notice that your DoT damage rises significantly during longer fights while melee stays consistent.

Bar Chart

Groups seven colored bars for each battle, showing:

  • Melee damage
  • Direct damage (spells)
  • DoT damage
  • Pet damage
  • Critical damage
  • Healing
  • Critical healing

Bar charts make it easy to compare the relative contribution of each damage type within and across battles.

Pie Chart

Shows how your damage was distributed across different targets by name for the entire session — it is cumulative across all battles recorded since your last reset, not just the most recent fight. Each slice represents a target, sized by the total damage you have dealt to it across all those battles. This is particularly useful to see where your damage has gone over a longer play session.

Controls

At the top of the Graphs tab, you will find a checkbox for each graph type. Check or uncheck them to show or hide individual graphs.

Each graph has its own scroll bar and navigation:

ControlWhat It Does
Scroll barNavigate through your battle history (shows "Battles X-Y of Z")

When the scroll bar is moved all the way to the right, the graph automatically follows the newest battles as they come in. If you scroll back to look at earlier battles, it stops auto-following until you return the slider to the far right.

Graphs display the last 50 battles in view at a time, with up to 250 battles stored in history. Older battles cycle out as new ones are recorded.
Enable just the DPS Line Graph for a clean, minimal view during grinding sessions. Turn on all four graphs when you want a comprehensive breakdown after a raid or named fight.
