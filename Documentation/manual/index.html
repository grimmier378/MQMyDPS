Welcome to MQMyDPS

MQMyDPS is a personal DPS and healing tracker for EverQuest. It monitors your damage output, healing, and combat statistics in real time, giving you detailed breakdowns per target, per battle, and across your entire session. Whether you want to see your DPS at a glance, watch floating damage numbers over your targets, or review historical graphs of your performance, MQMyDPS has you covered.

Quick Start

  1. Load the plugin — Type /plugin MQMyDPS in your EverQuest chat window.
  2. Open the main window — Type /mydps show to bring up the DPS Report window.
  3. Start tracking — Click the Start control in the menu bar (it becomes a Stop control while tracking), or type /mydps start. You are now tracking damage and healing.
MQMyDPS saves your settings automatically for each character on each server. Your configuration will be waiting for you next time you log in.

What Can It Do?

  • Live DPS tracking with per-target damage breakdown
  • Battle history with sortable, expandable tables
  • Healing tracking with per-player totals and pie charts
  • Floating combat text — 3D damage numbers anchored to targets in the game world
  • Combat overlay — a compact, real-time damage feed
  • Graphs and charts — DPS trends, damage breakdowns, bar charts, and pie charts
  • Peer sharing — see all your boxed characters' DPS and healing together, with All/Server/Group/Raid filtering
  • Pop-out tabs — detach any tab into its own movable window
  • Full color customization and 11 visual themes
  • Customizable chat patterns — edit how damage and healing messages are parsed, per server
  • Macro integration — access your DPS data from macros and Lua scripts
