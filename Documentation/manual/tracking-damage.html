Tracking Damage

The main DPS Report window organizes your combat data across several tabs: Current Battle, History, Targets, and Healing (described below), plus a Graphs tab and a Peers tab for your boxed characters, each covered on its own page.

Pop out any tab. Click the tab you want, then right-click the tab bar and choose Pop Out to move that tab into its own window you can position anywhere. Close that window to dock the tab back in the main window.

Current Battle

This tab shows live statistics for the fight in progress. The table adapts its columns to fit the window width, showing as many of these as space allows:

ColumnWhat It Shows
DPSDamage per second for this battle
DurationHow long the fight has lasted
TotalTotal damage dealt
AvgAverage damage per hit
HitsNumber of successful hits
CritsNumber of critical strikes
DoTsDamage from damage-over-time spells
PetDamage dealt by your pet
Non-MeleeDirect damage from spells
HealsTotal healing done
Crit HealsHealing from critical heals

Below the stats, two additional tables show:

  • Targets — Each target you are fighting, with their name, your DPS against them, hit count, and total damage
  • Healed Players — Each player you have healed, with their name, number of heals, and total healing
When no battle is active, this tab displays a message indicating there is no current combat data.

History

The History tab keeps a record of all completed battles during your session. Each battle appears as a row in a sortable table with up to 13 columns:

ColumnWhat It Shows
Battle #Sequential battle number
DPSOverall DPS for that battle
DurationTotal fight time
AvgAverage damage per hit
MeleeMelee damage total
CritsCritical strike damage
DoTsDoT damage total
PetPet damage total
Non-MeleeSpell direct damage
HealsHealing total
Crit HealsCritical heal total
Total DamageAll damage combined
Total HealsAll healing combined

You can customize this table in several ways:

  • Sort — Click any column header to sort by that column
  • Reorder columns — Drag column headers to rearrange them
  • Hide columns — Right-click a column header to show or hide columns
  • Resize columns — Drag the edges of column headers
  • Expand battles — Click a battle row to expand it and see per-target breakdowns and healed players
Use the Sort Newest First option in Settings to control whether the most recent battle appears at the top or bottom of the list.

Targets

The Targets tab provides two views:

  • Current Battle Targets — Every target in your active fight, showing DPS, hits, average damage, and total damage per target
  • All Session Targets — Accumulated damage to every target across all battles in your session

This is useful for seeing exactly how your damage distributes across multiple enemies in a pull, or your total session output against a specific mob type.

Healing

The Healing tab shows your session healing summary with a table listing each player you have healed:

ColumnWhat It Shows
PlayerName of the player you healed
Direct HealsTotal from non-critical heals
Crit HealsTotal from critical heals
CountNumber of heal casts
TotalDirect (non-critical) healing total for that player (critical heals are shown in their own column and are not added into this Total)

A pie chart below the table visualizes how your healing was distributed across players, making it easy to see who received the most attention.

How Battles Work

MQMyDPS automatically detects when you enter and leave combat:

  1. Combat begins when you deal damage to an enemy (misses do not count).
  2. Combat is active as long as you are fighting. All damage, healing, and related events are recorded.
  3. Pending end — When the game reports you are no longer in combat, MQMyDPS waits a configurable delay (default: 10 seconds) before finalizing the battle. This prevents brief pauses between pulls from splitting one fight into two.
  4. Battle finalized — After the delay, the battle is saved to your History and a summary report is generated.
If battles are splitting too often, increase the Battle End Delay in Settings. If they are merging too many pulls together, decrease it.

Pet Tracking

When the Track Pet option is enabled (on by default), MQMyDPS includes your pet's damage in your totals. Your pet's melee and spell damage are combined into a single Pet total, shown in Current Battle, History, and Graphs.
